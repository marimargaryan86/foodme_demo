## Execution problems

- **Dish cards:** a `ref` click on a dish card opened the dialog only once in five tries (Crispy Salad); Chuka Wakame Salad and every Mushroom soup card needed a click by coordinates from a screenshot.
- **Header link:** the `ref` click on **Explore chefs** didn't navigate; a coordinate click did (known pitfall).
- **Remove item:** the `ref` click silently did nothing; a coordinate click worked (known pitfall, run 19).
- **Chef pages load slowly:** 4 s after `navigate` wasn't enough (empty main); 6–8 s was.
- **`get_page_text` on a chef page** returns the whole ~150-dish menu; `find` is enough there.
- **Admin notifications:** "Order status updated" was missed twice (read after 4 s, or the update took longer than 1 s); caught once when read 1 s after the click. Status and buttons always matched.
- **Field values:** `read_page` shows placeholders, not input values; values were confirmed with `form_input`'s "previous" value and zoomed screenshots.
- **Tool approval:** one `browser_batch` was refused once (auto-mode classifier timeout); the retry a moment later worked. No order was affected.
